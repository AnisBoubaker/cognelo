# Student groupings and teams

Status: grouping management, student visibility controls, recorded assignment methods, capacity-aware random assignment, and student team selection implemented. Smart assignment is a disabled placeholder. [Activity-specific exceptions](ACTIVITY_ASSIGNMENT_EXCEPTIONS.md) are implemented; collaborative activity execution remains a future integration.

## Model and behavior

The hierarchy is **course → section (`CourseGroup`) → grouping (`StudentGrouping`) → teams (`StudentTeam`) → student enrollments (`StudentTeamMember`)**. A section can have independent groupings such as Project teams, Lab partners, and Exam arrangements. Grouping titles are unique within a section. Team titles are unique within their grouping (case-insensitive validation), and surrounding whitespace is trimmed.

A student can belong to at most one team in each grouping, but can have a different assignment in every grouping. Unassigned students, empty teams, and empty groupings are valid. Membership references a section participant, including pending enrollments without an activated account. Teachers and TAs are never eligible team members. Cross-section membership and team IDs from another grouping are rejected.

Each team has a stable ID that survives renaming and membership edits. The database's composite membership key enforces one team per grouping per participant, and the composite team foreign key ensures that a membership's team belongs to its grouping. Service validation checks the participant's section and student role.

## Permissions and interface

Administrators and explicit course owners/teachers manage groupings in any section they manage. A section teacher may manage groupings only in their assigned section. TAs, learners, and unrelated staff cannot use the teacher grouping-management endpoints. An enrolled student can use the separate **My teams** projection in their own published, currently available section. Visible manual/random groupings show the grouping name and only the student’s own team with teammate names. Visible choice-enabled groupings show every team and its member names/available slots so students can choose. Grouping descriptions, student email addresses, enrollment IDs, revisions, and audit data are never included in this learner projection. Staff roles do not bypass the enrollment requirement on learner endpoints.

Course managers use **Course → Groupings**, then select a section. Section teachers use the **Groupings** tab in their section workspace. The panel loads only when opened. Course managers have one compact toolbar containing the section selector and Create grouping/Close action; the redundant Groupings title and help text are omitted. Saved groupings start collapsed, with at most one open at a time, using the same compact folder rows, circular disclosure indicator, and actions menu as Participants. Changing section or reloading closes all groupings. Opening reveals the description, choice schedule, team cards, and unassigned count; team count, assignment method, and student visibility remain visible in collapsed rows. Editing or saving opens that grouping when returning to the list. The editor saves the grouping name, optional description, teams, and all assignments together. It supports student search, an unassigned-only filter, explicit unassigned choices, and visible team sizes. Saved groupings use separate team cards with top-aligned student lists, decorative initials, and member-count badges. The section selector stays compact, and the cards reflow into one column on narrow screens. Edit and Remove are available in each row’s actions menu, and removal keeps its existing confirmation. English, French, Chinese, and Arabic copy is maintained in the host application.

**Show grouping to students** controls visibility independently of the assignment method. New and existing groupings default to visible. Hidden groupings are excluded from learner responses, including a student's own team; direct choice requests return 404. Hiding preserves all teams, memberships, capacities, dates, and change permissions. A hidden student-choice grouping cannot accept choices; revealing it resumes the saved policy only if its selection window permits. Visibility changes apply on Save, share the revision/concurrency checks, and appear in staff audit snapshots. A student's open My teams panel removes hidden groupings on its next refresh or focus.

The main editor and saved cards show **Manual**, **Random**, or **Let students choose**, plus student visibility. Student-choice summaries include From/Until (or Immediately/No deadline), change permission, and a paused notice while hidden. The editor's **Use manual assignment** action disables self-selection and clears the choice dates/change permission while retaining all teams and memberships. Adjusting a team membership directly preserves the selected policy; a teacher can refine a random assignment or student-choice grouping without implicitly changing its method.

Adding a team prefills an editable name in the course subject’s teaching language: `Team 1`, `Équipe 1`, `团队 1`, or `الفريق 1`. The next number starts at the current team count plus one and skips names already used (ignoring case and surrounding whitespace). Existing and custom names remain unchanged after additions, removals, reloads, or language changes. Team-name inputs have accessible labels without visible field headings. Interface and accessible labels still follow the viewer’s locale.

**Automatic assign** opens a dialog with Random, Smart (disabled, coming soon based on student progression), and Let students choose. Every team has its own integer capacity from 0 to 5,000, defaulting to four. A shortcut applies one capacity to all teams; individual overrides remain possible. Zero slots closes a team to new members. Capacities are checked on every teacher save and student choice; a teacher cannot save fewer slots than the team’s current membership.

Random assignment offers **unassigned students only** (preserves existing memberships) and **reassign all students** (replaces memberships). It shuffles eligible section students and fills the smallest non-full teams, with random tie breaking. Equal capacities produce balanced teams when redistributing everyone. A capacity shortfall leaves remaining students unassigned; it never creates additional teams or exceeds a capacity. Pending enrollments participate, roster filters are ignored and cleared, and team IDs/names remain unchanged. Other groupings are unaffected.

Student choice supports optional From and Until dates, entered in the viewer’s local time and stored as UTC instants, plus permission to change a confirmed choice. With no From date it opens immediately; with no Until date it has no deadline. If both dates are present, Until must be strictly later than From. The selection interval includes From and **excludes Until: at `now >= choiceClosesAt`, students cannot choose or change teams even when changes are allowed**. Closing does not remove memberships. Before opening, after closing, or once a no-change choice is confirmed, cards remain visible but read-only. Teachers can still manage assignments and reopen or adjust the window.

Dialog Apply modifies only the grouping draft. The ordinary Save publishes visibility, assignment method, capacities, choice settings, and assignments together; Cancel restores the saved grouping. Choosing Random records the random method and turns off student self-selection when saved. Smart assignment has no executable behavior or accepted API value.

Learners use **Section → My teams**. Choice cards show names, member counts/capacities, and open slots; very large teams abbreviate slots beyond twenty with a remaining count. Selecting a card stages a choice; **Confirm choice** is required to persist it. Changing teams moves the membership atomically, keeping the old team if the new one is full or a concurrent update wins. The panel refreshes availability every 15 seconds while visible and on focus, disables selection locally at the closing instant, and relies on server checks for authority. Requests that lose a capacity race reload the teams and ask the student to review and confirm again.

One draft owns the shared Save/Cancel action bar and unsaved-navigation guard. Successful creation and subsequent saves show localized confirmations through the app-wide notification service, including its notification history. The same save handler reports success when saving through the unsaved-navigation guard; failed or cancelled saves never show a success notification. Cancel restores the saved snapshot; Close confirms discarding unsaved work. A failed save retains the draft. A stale save or delete shows a recoverable conflict, with an explicit reload action that confirms before discarding edits. Section selection is disabled while an editor is open.

## API and concurrency

Routes are under `/api/courses/:courseId/groups/:groupId/groupings`:

- `GET /`: returns `{ groupings, students }` for the authorized section.
- `POST /`: creates a grouping and optional teams; returns `{ grouping }` with status 201.
- `PATCH /:groupingId`: replaces the grouping's editable composition using its current positive integer `revision`.
- `DELETE /:groupingId`: accepts `{ revision }` and deletes the grouping and its teams, preserving student enrollments.

Create/update input contains `title`, optional `description`, `visibleToStudents`, `assignmentMethod` (`manual`, `random`, or `student_choice`), `studentChoiceEnabled`, nullable ISO `choiceOpensAt` / `choiceClosesAt`, `allowChoiceChanges`, and `teams: [{ id?, title, capacity, participantIds }]`. An explicit assignment method is authoritative: the compatibility `studentChoiceEnabled` flag is normalized from it. Older clients that only send the choice flag map to student choice or manual. A database check keeps the method and flag consistent. Creation defaults to visible, manual assignment, no dates, changes disabled, and four slots per team. Omitting visibility on update preserves its stored value, so an older client cannot inadvertently reveal a hidden grouping. Creation accepts new teams only; on update, retained teams must supply their existing IDs. Omitting an existing team removes it. Title length is limited to 120 characters, description to 2,000, teams to 500, and members per team to 5,000. No full-roster assignment is required.

Writes run in serializable transactions. Updates and deletes compare the supplied revision; updates increment it. A conflicting edit, transaction serialization failure, or concurrent foreign-key change returns 409. Duplicate grouping names also return 409. Invalid team/member composition returns 400; an authorized request for a section outside the URL course, or a grouping outside the URL section, returns 404. Actor-attributed `CourseAuditEvent` snapshots record grouping creation, update, and deletion in the same transaction.

Learner routes are under `/api/courses/:courseId/groups/:groupId/my-teams`:

- `GET /`: returns only visible groupings in `{ groupings }`, with the restricted name/team projection and `choiceState` (`disabled`, `upcoming`, `open`, `locked`, or `closed`).
- `POST /:groupingId`: accepts only `{ teamId }`, derives enrollment from the signed-in user, and returns `{ ok: true }`. Client-supplied participant identities are rejected. Foreign section/grouping/team IDs never grant access.

Student writes check enrollment, section availability, grouping visibility, selection dates, change permission, and team capacity inside a serializable transaction. Each successful new choice or move increments the grouping revision before upserting the single membership, so a teacher’s stale full-draft save cannot overwrite student choices. Competing last-slot requests cannot overfill a team. Closed/upcoming/disabled selection and locked changes return 403; full teams or transaction conflicts return 409; hidden/foreign groupings and foreign teams return 404. An idempotent confirmation of the current team is allowed only while selection is open (or locked), never after Until or while hidden. Actor-attributed `student_team_chosen` audit entries record the previous/new team in the same transaction.

## Lifecycle and deployment

Migration `202610120001_student_groupings` adds three core tables without changing existing participant or activity data. Migration `202610120002_student_team_choice` adds capacities and choice policies. Existing team capacities are backfilled to the greater of four or their current member count, preserving all assignments; existing groupings stay manual. Apply migrations with the standard `npm run db:migrate:all` workflow. Restart running API processes after generating the changed Prisma client.

Migration `202610120003_student_grouping_visibility` adds visibility and the assignment-method enum/check. Existing groupings remain visible and choice-enabled groupings are backfilled to `student_choice`. Other existing groupings are labeled `manual`: earlier randomization did not record its method, so historical random assignments cannot be inferred. Memberships are unchanged. New random assignments record the method explicitly.

Unenrollment cascades the student's team memberships and individual activity exceptions; the grouping and teams remain. An open editor attempting to restore a removed enrollment fails membership validation and must reload. Deleting a grouping never unenrolls students. Deleting a section cascades its groupings and teams. The existing section-delete-and-move-roster action also deletes that section's groupings; teams are not transferred into an unrelated section's grouping structure. Activity exception rules reference stable team IDs and cascade when those teams are deleted. Deleting a team or grouping preserves existing attempts, grades, and their audit history. See [Activity assignment exceptions](ACTIVITY_ASSIGNMENT_EXCEPTIONS.md).

Activity exceptions define precedence and timing snapshots in their [canonical policy](ACTIVITY_ASSIGNMENT_EXCEPTIONS.md). Creating a grouping alone does not alter activities; a teacher must add its team exceptions in activity settings. Future collaborative work still needs explicit team attempt, shared submission, ownership, grading, and archival policies.
